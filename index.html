The Framework

Six dimensions.
One standard.

Every person in the system is measured across six behavioral dimensions — not once, but continuously — so excellence isn't assumed. It's built, tracked, and verified.

Star 1
Craft

The quality of the work itself. Skills, output, and domain knowledge — assessed and on record.

Star 2
Integrity

Values in action, not just on paper. Ethics scenario assessments — three situations, all three must pass.

Star 3
Communication

How you listen, respond, and show up for others — assessed through real scenarios and ongoing interaction data.

Star 4
Reliability

Follow-through, consistency, and dependability — tracked across real commitments and engagements.

Star 5
Experience

The impact you leave on others — measured through structured feedback from the people you work with and serve.

Star 6
Legacy

How you invest in others, give back, and grow beyond yourself — the long-term mark of a person who truly operates at six stars.
